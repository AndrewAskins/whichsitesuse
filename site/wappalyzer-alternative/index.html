// 02  Shared heritage

How are the detection rules related?

WhichSitesUse's detections come from HTTP Archive's fork of the open-source Wappalyzer rules, so the two share a starting point.

HTTP Archive keeps its own copy of the rules that the Wappalyzer project published as open source, and runs it on every monthly crawl. That fork covers about 3,900 technologies in 108 categories (see the fork on GitHub), and WhichSitesUse reads its results, so the technology names and categories will look familiar if you've used Wappalyzer. A lookup on allbirds.com in the November 2026 crawl, for example, which our homepage shows, lists 32 technologies under names like Shopify, Shop Pay, Vue.js, Tailwind CSS, Microsoft Clarity, Attentive and Google Tag Manager.

HTTP Archive maintains the fork and Wappalyzer maintains its commercial product, though, so a technology one of them recognises may be missing from the other, and two detections for the same site can disagree. We haven't measured how large that gap is, so if one technology matters a lot to you, look up a few sites you know run it before you buy a list.

// 03  Cost

On WhichSitesUse, a list of 500 Shopify stores is 500 credits, about $1.24 on the Growth plan.

Counts, technology search, balance checks and "not covered" lookups are free, so count_sites can tell you how many of the 756,868 Shopify sites in the November 2026 crawl match your filters, and what the full list would cost, before you spend anything.

Wappalyzer prices by plan instead. // 04  For agents

Built for agents and scripts to call

Wappalyzer has a hosted MCP server too, so what's different here is the billing and the spend controls rather than access.

  1. find_technologies matches a plain name to the exact technology for free, and flags cases where two products share a name.
  2. count_sites says how many sites match and what the full list would cost, also for free.
  3. search_sites pulls the list at one credit per site and stops at the max_credits limit you set.
  4. lookup_domain returns one domain's stack for 2 credits, or nothing charged when it isn't covered.
  5. buy_credits sends a person a checkout link to approve, because an agent can't pay on its own.

Responses are typed and paginated, and each one carries the crawl date, the credits used and the balance left. Connection details for Claude, Cursor and other MCP clients are in the docs.

// 05  Where Wappalyzer wins

When is Wappalyzer the better buy?

If one of these is what you're paying for, Wappalyzer will serve you better than we can.

You need people to contact

Wappalyzer's lists and API can include company details, emails, phone numbers and social profiles, and it verifies email addresses. WhichSitesUse returns domains and what they run, with no contact or company data at all.

You want the data inside your CRM

Wappalyzer enriches Salesforce, HubSpot and Pipedrive records and connects to Zapier, Make and Pabbly. We have no CRM integrations, so you'd move a CSV or call the API yourself.

You check sites as you browse

The Wappalyzer extension shows a page's stack while you're on it. We have a free lookup on the homepage and nothing that runs in your browser.

You need fresher or wider coverage

Wappalyzer says it re-verifies technologies at least monthly and uses extension data to reach pages ordinary crawlers miss. Our data is a monthly HTTP Archive snapshot that can be up to about six weeks old, and sites with little Chrome traffic are often missing from it.

// 07  Questions

Common questions

Is Wappalyzer still free?

Partly. A free Wappalyzer account includes 50 lookups and email verifications a month, 5 website alerts and sample lead lists, and lead lists, APIs and CRM integrations need a paid plan (checked November 2026 on wappalyzer.com/pricing).

Does WhichSitesUse use Wappalyzer's data?

No. Detections come from HTTP Archive's public monthly crawl, which runs HTTP Archive's own fork of the open-source Wappalyzer rules. WhichSitesUse doesn't buy or resell anything from Wappalyzer's service.

Is there a WhichSitesUse browser extension?

No. The closest thing is the free lookup on the WhichSitesUse homepage, which needs no account. If you want a stack readout on every page you visit, Wappalyzer's extension is the better tool for that.

Will technology counts match Wappalyzer's?

Not exactly, because the two use different crawls and separately maintained rules. WhichSitesUse counts sites in HTTP Archive's crawl of about 15 million sites with real Chrome traffic, so a count is a share of that crawl rather than of the whole web.
